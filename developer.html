Founder ZISHU TRON

Meet the developer behind ZishuChat.

Zishan is a student who builds software, apps and digital products, and shares the work through ZishuTron.

Founder and developer

Zishan

Zishan is a student who spends most of his time learning, experimenting and building software. His interest in technology began with simple curiosity about how apps and websites work.

ZishuTron is his long-term effort to create useful technology, from AI-powered tools to web applications and digital experiences. ZishuChat is written in Java and built with Zishu IDE, ZishuTron's own Android code editor and build tool.
