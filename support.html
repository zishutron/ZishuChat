Quick help

The most common questions

Messages or calls arrive late

Some phones stop apps when you swipe them away. In your phone settings allow ZishuChat to run without battery restrictions, turn on Autostart or Background activity, and lock the app in recent apps.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password and enter your email. We send a link so you can choose a new password. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.

Delete your account

Open Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. For security you may be asked to confirm your password. Need help? Email us from the address on your account.

Report abuse or a problem user

Open the chat menu and choose Report. Your mail app opens with the details filled in. You can also block the person from the same menu.

Calls will not connect

Check that both phones have internet and that camera and microphone permissions are on. If the problem continues, send us your phone model and what the call screen shows.

Photos and files

Files can be up to 10 MB. They are uploaded to third-party hosting, so please never share ID cards, bank details or anything private. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Troubleshooting

More answers

How do I find someone on ZishuChat?

Search by exact email or start typing a username. You can also scan their QR code from Contacts or the Profile tab. The app does not read your phone's address book.

I cannot open my locked chats.

Locked chats use your phone screen lock. Make sure a PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint is set in your phone settings. Then open Locked chats from the Chats menu, Settings, or by pressing and holding the Chats tab.

Can I use two accounts on one phone?

Yes, up to five. Open Profile, then Switch account. Notifications and calls arrive for the account that is currently active.

Why do disappearing messages still exist on the server?

Messages older than the time you pick are hidden for both people, but they are not erased from the server. This is described in the Privacy Policy.

Are my chats end-to-end encrypted?

No. Connections use HTTPS, but messages are stored on the service's servers and are not end-to-end encrypted. Please share carefully.

Is there an iPhone version?

ZishuChat is available for Android. Other platforms have not been announced.
