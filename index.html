FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is ZishuChat free?

Yes. Messaging and voice and video calls are free to use, and the app shows no ads.

Which phones does it run on?

Any Android phone running Android 5.0 or newer. Other platforms have not been announced yet.

How do I find my friends?

Search by username or an exact email address, scan their QR code, or share yours from the Profile tab. The app does not read your phone's address book.

Are my chats end-to-end encrypted?

No. Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections and access is limited by security rules, but messages are stored on the service's servers. Shared photos and files are uploaded to third-party hosting. Please do not share anything you would not want others to see. The Privacy Policy has the full details.

How do Locked chats work?

Press and hold a chat, tap the three dots and choose Lock chat. It disappears from your lists and opens only after you confirm your phone screen lock. Locked chats also sync to your other phones signed in to the same account.

Can I use more than one account?

Yes, up to five on the same phone. Notifications and calls arrive for the account that is currently active.

What are the file and voice limits?

Files and photos can be up to 10 MB each. A single voice message can run up to five minutes.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. Your profile, username and sign-in are removed. Messages already delivered to other people may remain in their chats.

How do I reach support?

Visit the Support page or email us at support.stardustofficial@gmail.com. We read every message.
