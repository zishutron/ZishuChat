Who we are

A long-term effort to make technology more useful and more human.

ZishuTron is an independent technology brand. We build software, applications and digital tools, and we care most about products that are useful, well designed and open to everyone.

Our work spans Android apps, web applications, AI-powered tools, cloud-based services and developer tools. Our products share one vision, one account and one design language, so they feel like parts of the same ecosystem.
