This Privacy Policy explains what information ZishuChat ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information: your e-mail address or phone number and the credentials used to sign in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in readable form.

Profile information: your display name, username, profile photo and short bio. This information is visible to other users of the app.

Contacts: the list of people you add or chat with inside the app. We do not read your phone's address book.

Messages and shared content: text messages, reactions, replies, group names and members, voice notes, photos, documents and the time they were sent, delivered and read.

Call information: who called whom, when, for how long, and whether the call was audio or video. The audio and video of a call travel directly between the devices, or through a relay (TURN) server when a direct connection is not possible. We do not record calls.

Presence information: whether you are online, your last-seen time and typing indicators. You can hide your last-seen time in Settings.

Device and technical information: a push-notification token, the app version and basic diagnostic information needed to deliver messages and keep the service working.

Permissions: the app asks for the camera (photos and video calls), microphone (voice notes and calls), notifications, and the ability to run in the background to deliver messages and calls. You choose files and photos yourself through the system picker; the app does not scan your gallery or storage.

2. How we use information

To create and secure your account; to let other people find you and chat with you; to deliver messages, calls and notifications; to show your profile, online status and read receipts to your contacts; to prevent abuse and keep the service stable; and to comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertisements.

3. How people can find you

Other users can find you by your username, or by your exact e-mail address or phone number. To make this possible without publishing them, the app stores only a one-way hash (a scrambled fingerprint) of your e-mail or phone number for look-ups. Your e-mail address and phone number are never shown to other users and are not stored in your public profile. Your username, name, photo, bio and online status are visible to other signed-in users. Usernames cannot be used to sign in.

4. Third-party services that process your data

The app is built on third-party services. When you use the app, data is processed by:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging): accounts, profiles, chats, messages, call signalling and push notifications. Data is stored on Google servers. See Google's privacy policy.
  • ImgBB (imgbb.com): photos you send or use as a profile picture are uploaded to this image-hosting service.
  • Cloudinary (cloudinary.com): voice notes, documents and (as a fallback) photos are uploaded to this media-hosting service.
  • Google STUN servers and a public TURN relay (Metered Open Relay): used only to connect audio and video calls.

These providers have their own terms and privacy policies, and we do not control how they operate.

5. Photos and files you share (important)

Photos and files that you share are uploaded to the third-party platforms listed above and are referenced by a web link. Anyone who has the link may be able to open the file, and the files are not end-to-end encrypted. Deleting a message in the app does not necessarily delete the uploaded file from the hosting platform.

Do NOT share private or personal photos, documents, identity cards, bank or card details, passwords or any content you would not want others to see. You are responsible for the content you share.

Locked chats

Locked chats are a convenience feature that hides a chat inside the app and opens it only after you confirm your phone screen lock. The app does not see or store your PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint; the check is done by your phone. The list of chats you locked, and your alert settings for locked chats, are saved in your private account data on the cloud so that your other phones show the same locked chats; only you can read it. Locked chats are NOT end-to-end encrypted and are not stored differently from other chats. Messages, photos, voice notes and files sent in a locked chat are stored on the same servers and uploaded to the same third-party platforms (ImgBB, Cloudinary and others listed above) as in any other chat. Locking a chat does not protect what the other person receives: they can save, forward or screenshot it. Do not share private or personal photos or files in locked chats and do not ask others to share them with you.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access to stored data is restricted by security rules. However, messages are NOT end-to-end encrypted: they are stored on the service's servers and could be accessed by the operator of the service or the infrastructure providers. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Deleting your account removes your profile, username, look-up records and sign-in. Messages you already sent to other people may remain visible to them in their chats, and files uploaded to third-party hosting platforms may remain available there until those providers remove them. You can ask for help with deletion by contacting us (see section 11).

8. Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, hide your last-seen time, turn message previews off, block users, and delete your account. Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data and to object to some processing. To exercise these rights, contact us.

9. Children

ZishuChat is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the law of your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children below that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email us at support.stardustofficial@gmail.com (also shown in the About screen).
